Introduction back to ToC

CCO is a domain-independent ontology that captures commonly occurring structural patterns found in existing compliance ontologies, including norms and their deontic types, prescribed actions, applicability conditions, exceptions, role-based applicability, and temporal scope. CCO also models the agents and roles involved in issuing and complying with regulations.
