CCO: A Core Compliance Ontology for Modelling the Normative Structure of Regulations: Description back to ToC

A domain-independent core ontology for modelling the regulatory structure of regulations across diverse compliance domains. It captures regulations, norms including obligations, permissions, and prohibitions, conditions, exceptions, actions, roles, agents, resources, and regulatory authority agents and roles. The ontology supports temporal modelling of norm applicability and role holding, while enabling domain-specific extensions for specialised compliance contexts.
